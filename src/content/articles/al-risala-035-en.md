*This is a short English overview of the Arabic summary of this session in Dr. Yasir Burhami's explanation of Imam al-Shafi'i's al-Risalah. For the full text, see the Arabic version on this site or the original post.*

Hadiths forbid praying after Fajr and 'Asr, and at sunrise, zenith and sunset. Other hadiths say that whoever catches one rak'ah before sunrise has caught Fajr, and that a forgotten prayer should be prayed as soon as it is remembered.

Al-Shafi'i reconciles these by limiting the ban to voluntary prayers that have no specific reason. Obligatory prayers may be prayed at these times. So may prayers with a cause, such as missed prayers, the two rak'ahs after tawaf and the funeral prayer. He notes that a person could not "catch" a prayer at a time when praying was forbidden altogether.

The Companions differed over praying the two rak'ahs of tawaf at these times: some delayed them, while others, including Ibn 'Umar, Ibn 'Abbas and 'A'ishah, prayed them. Al-Shafi'i takes this as a sign that the matter allows some room. He stresses, however, that what is established from the Prophet ﷺ binds everyone and is not overridden by any Companion's practice.
