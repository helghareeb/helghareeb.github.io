*This is a short English overview of the Arabic summary of this session in Dr. Yasir Burhami's explanation of Imam al-Shafi'i's al-Risalah. For the full text, see the Arabic version on this site or the original post.*

This session again puts harmonising texts before preferring one over another.

*Muzabanah* means trading dates still on the palm for dried dates by measure. It is forbidden because the quantities cannot be matched, which makes it uncertain and usurious. The *'araya* allowance permits a small version of this trade, below five *wasqs*, for people in need. Al-Shafi'i treats it as an exception carved out of the general ban, not a contradiction of it.

The hadith of Hakim ibn Hizam, "do not sell what you do not have", is limited to specific, identified goods. A *salam* sale is still valid. In such a sale, goods are described precisely and promised for a later date, and the full price is paid at the time of the contract. The commentary also distinguishes items owned but absent from items not owned at all. It allows an exception where precise description gives the buyer a right to cancel if the goods do not match.
