*This is a short English overview of the Arabic summary of this session in Dr. Yasir Burhami's explanation of Imam al-Shafi'i's al-Risalah. For the full text, see the Arabic version on this site or the original post.*

**The *quru'*.** The summary first completes the discussion from the previous session. It answers the argument drawn from a captive woman waiting a single menstrual cycle: that wait only confirms she is not pregnant and is not the waiting period of divorce. It again gives priority to the Prophet's ﷺ use of the word over linguistic arguments, and it repeats its preference for menstruation.

**The husband who swears off his wife.** The second topic is the *muli*, a husband who swears not to sleep with his wife. Some Companions held that divorce happens automatically once four months pass. Others held that he is then summoned and must either return to her or divorce her. Al-Shafi'i prefers the second view. The verse (Al-Baqarah 2:226-227) mentions returning and deciding on divorce together, after the four months.

He also rejects comparing the oath with a deadline for finishing a building. A deadline requires the work to be done within the time. The oath does not turn the passing of the four months into a divorce.
