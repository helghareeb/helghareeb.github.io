### Lecture Title: Conclusion of the Course “Reading Ancient Manuscripts” - Discussions and Questions

### Main Elements

#### Introduction

- The lecture opened with greetings and supplication.
- It was noted that this was the closing lecture of the course.
- The importance of interaction and of gathering questions to be answered during the session was stressed.

#### Topics of Discussion

- **Addressing problems and questions:**
  - Inviting attendees to put forward their questions about manuscripts.
  - Dealing with some common problems, such as the positions of dots and marks that may seem unclear.
- **Observations on copying methodology:**
  - The importance of studying the whole copy (nuskha) in order to identify recurring phenomena.
  - Explaining that dots and marks appearing in copies may result from the ink or the passage of time.
  - A caution against relying on conclusions based on phenomena that do not recur.
- **Copyists' correction of errors:**
  - Discussing how copyists dealt with errors in texts.
  - The difference between a clear correction and additions that require investigation.
- **Studying the context:**
  - Stressing the importance of examining the context in order to understand unclear passages.
  - Urging editors to consult other copies to clarify meanings.

#### Interactive Discussions

- Practical examples presented by attendees concerning their questions and observations.
- Discussion of distinctive marks in manuscripts, such as “صح” (sahh, “correct”) or the symbols used for confirmation.
- Offering methodological guidance for analyzing textual phenomena.

#### Closing Advice

- The importance of continuous practice in the study of manuscripts.
- Encouraging participants to collect problems and seek solutions for them together with colleagues.
- The next course will focus on dealing with the non-original texts in manuscripts.

#### Thanks and Appreciation

- Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari thanked the participants for their good engagement.
- A supplication for success and for continuing the work of editing manuscripts.

### Summary

The lecture focused on engaging with practical problems in reading and analyzing manuscripts, and offered important methodological guidance that can benefit editors in their future work.
