**Lecture title: “Investigating Differences Between Copies and Analyzing the Method of the Scholars”**

**Introduction**

Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari opened the lecture with a warm welcome to the attendees, highlighting the importance of the critical editing of manuscripts (tahqiq al-makhtutat) as a fundamental pillar for protecting the Islamic heritage from distortion. He stressed that this discipline is not merely a technical effort, but a cultural and intellectual treasure that requires advanced analytical and methodological tools.

**Core Elements of the Lecture**

### 1. The Role of Scholarly Discussion in Developing Knowledge

- The Shaykh stressed that scholarly discussions are not merely teaching tools; they are engines for producing ideas and deepening understanding.
- He pointed out that good questions shed light on unexplored aspects, which strengthens the critical analysis of texts.
- He called for active academic interaction between the lecturer and the trainees, stressing that this helps broaden their understanding.

### 2. Causes of Differences Between Manuscript Copies

- The Shaykh discussed the factors that contribute to differences between copies (nusakh, sing. nuskha), dividing them into internal and external factors:
  - **Variation in the skills of copyists:** differences in how well copyists (nussakh, sing. nasikh) had mastered the language affected the accuracy of the copy.
  - **The purposes of copying:** some copies were prepared for teaching or personal purposes, which led to variation in quality.
  - **Curricular texts:** such as *Nukhbat al-Fikar* and *Anwar al-Tanzil*, which were copied in great numbers, increasing the likelihood of differences.
- The Shaykh stressed that tampering with original texts strips them of their scholarly value.

### 3. Methodological Approaches to Dealing with Differences

- He reviewed the method of the scholars in dealing with textual errors, citing the efforts of Ibn Marzuq:
  - **Precise documentation:** the importance of recording corrections in the margins without altering the original text.
  - **Analytical criticism:** the need for a critical vision to distinguish additions from the original texts.

### 4. The Phenomenon of Tashif and Forgery in Copies

- The Shaykh explained that misreading of dots and letter-shapes (tashif) usually results from unintentional errors, such as similarity in sound.
- He pointed out that claims of forgery are sometimes used to support particular agendas, which calls for methodical scrutiny to refute such claims.

### 5. The Importance of Commentaries and Editors' Introductions

- The Shaykh highlighted the role of commentaries (shuruh) in shedding light on the details of texts and offering clarifying readings.
- He cited the introductions of editors such as Ahmad Shakir and Abd al-Rahman al-Mu'allimi, pointing out their importance in identifying the differences between copies.

### 6. Applied Models for Analyzing Texts

- The Shaykh presented practical examples from books such as *al-Mughni* by Ibn Qudama, showing how the existence of multiple authorial versions (ibrazat) affected variation in the texts.
- He stressed the importance of historical understanding and comparative analysis for uncovering the roots of the differences.

### 7. Editors' Methods for Handling Differences Between Copies

- He discussed the method of Shaykh Ahmad Shakir, which relies on:
  - Choosing the most reliable copies.
  - Recording differences only in places of importance.
  - Disregarding minor errors that do not affect the text.
- The Shaykh stressed that this approach strengthens the focus on the essence of the texts.

### 8. The Importance of Continuous Practice and Extensive Reading

- The Shaykh called for sustained reading in the field of manuscripts in order to achieve deep understanding.
- He stressed that continuous reading strengthens the critical faculty, helping the editor analyze texts effectively.

**Conclusion**

Shaykh Salih concluded the lecture by stressing that critical editing requires a combination of precision and creativity. He recommended reading *Anmat al-Tawthiq fi al-Makhtut* (Patterns of Documentation in the Manuscript) by Abid Sulayman al-Mashukhi, considering it an important reference for understanding the accompanying texts. He stressed that serious work and perseverance are the best way to develop this vital field and to serve the Islamic heritage.
