### Title: Problems in Reading and Editing Arabic Manuscripts: The Comparative Method and Textual Analysis

### Introduction

This lesson, delivered by Shaykh Salih al-Azhari, deals with the fine and complex issues involved in editing Arabic manuscripts (makhtutat), a task that requires extreme precision and a comprehensive understanding of the environment in which these texts were produced. The Shaykh focuses on uncovering the problems the editor faces during the work, highlighting the importance of understanding the conduct of copyists (nussakh) and its effects, analyzing obscure texts, and using precise comparative methods to analyze texts and determine the most credible original.

### Main Elements of the Lesson

#### 1. Managing Copyists' Errors and Textual Corrections

- The Shaykh explained the importance of being aware of the common errors copyists fall into, which may result from misunderstanding, misreading, or even their intention to improve the text. These errors include substituting similar-looking letters, omitting dots, or adding letters not present in the original text.
- He stressed the need to adopt a careful analytical approach in which the written form (rasm) is given preference if there are indicators supporting it, and to avoid hastily judging texts to be wrong without clear evidence.
- He called for studying the historical and cultural contexts of copyists in order to know how these factors may affect the way they copied texts.

#### 2. Broadening the Use of Copies and External Sources

- The Shaykh stressed that comparing different copies (nusakh) and parallel sources is a fundamental pillar of editing texts accurately. Analyzing the differences between copies helps trace the origin of texts and understand the transformations they went through.
- He mentioned the importance of researching the biographies of the figures connected with the texts, in order to widen the circle of knowledge and help verify the names and events mentioned.
- Using secondary sources such as works of history and biographical dictionaries (tarajim) enriches the understanding of texts and provides a wider context for the editor.

#### 3. The Method for Dealing with Obscure Texts

- The Shaykh called for dealing patiently with obscure texts, while trying to understand them within their general contexts. In such cases, consulting the different copies becomes extremely important.
- He recommended involving the reader in understanding the problems related to the texts by recording observations and the different possibilities in the footnotes (hawashi).
- He stressed the importance of distinguishing between obscurity resulting from copyists' errors and obscurity resulting from the author's style, since each requires a different method of analysis.

#### 4. Balancing Between Tashif and the Correct Reading

- The Shaykh pointed out that a common misreading of dots and letter-shapes (tashif) may be evidence of a shared original behind the texts if it recurs in multiple copies. Analyzing these errors can reveal the relationships between different copies.
- He explained the importance of carefully verifying the differences between the various copies in order to determine the most correct text, while recognizing that a slight change in a single letter can lead to a great difference in meaning.
- He added that the study of tashif can be a tool for analyzing copyists' methods and understanding the nature of their work.

#### 5. Editing Poetic Texts

- The Shaykh stressed that poetic texts require a deep understanding of prosody ('ilm al-'arud) in order to detect possible errors in meter or rhyme. If the errors are found in the author's own hand, they must be retained as part of the original text.
- He pointed out that errors produced by copyists should be recorded in the footnotes while the base text is left unchanged, which ensures scholarly integrity in presenting the texts.
- He advised editors to distinguish between spontaneous errors and systematic changes in poetic texts.

#### 6. Dealing with Differences Between Copies

- The Shaykh explained that agreement of the copies on a particular reading is strong evidence of the soundness of the text, whereas their disagreement requires analysis of the copies and other sources to establish the correct reading.
- He stressed the importance of verifying the origins of errors and determining whether they arose from the copyists or from the author himself.
- He added that understanding the cultural and linguistic contexts helps clarify the reasons for the differences between copies.

#### 7. The Conduct of Copyists and Its Effect on Texts

- The Shaykh pointed out that copyists may make changes to texts based on their personal understanding, which may lead to distortion (tahrif) of the texts. He recommended dealing with such conduct with great caution.
- He stressed the importance of analyzing copyists' motives and the effect of their conduct on the texts, and of employing this for a deeper understanding of the text.
- He stressed the need to distinguish between additions intended to clarify the text and those that change its substance.

#### 8. The Importance of the Editor's Independent Reading

- The Shaykh explained that the editor must be able to rely on himself in analyzing texts, without excessive reliance on notes or commentaries.
- He pointed out that independent reading and continuous research are the foundation for developing analytical skills and the ability to deal with textual problems.
- He called for strengthening editors' intellectual independence, enabling them to devise solutions to the problems they face during editing.

### Summary

Shaykh Salih al-Azhari presented a comprehensive framework for dealing with the issues of manuscript editing. He focused on the importance of deliberation before passing judgments and of adopting a precise method of analysis and comparison, while adhering to the ethics of scholarly editing. He also stressed the importance of continuous reading and practical work in order to reach a professional level in this field.

This lesson offers an in-depth vision that requires high analytical skills and precision in dealing with texts, which makes commitment and diligent work essential for success in this field.

### Recommendations

- Strengthening the study of manuscripts and broadening knowledge of the historical and cultural contexts connected with them.
- Developing precise comparative methods between the different copies in order to arrive at the most accurate texts.
- Intensifying research into the biographies of figures and into historical sources in order to understand the full context of the texts.
- Developing linguistic and prosodic skills in order to understand poetic texts more deeply.
- Strengthening independent reading and practical work in order to achieve excellence in editing.

### The Call to Application

Shaykh Salih al-Azhari stressed the need to translate theoretical knowledge into practical application. He called on editors to practice comparing copies and analyzing texts themselves, pointing out that practice is the only way to master the discipline of editing. He also stressed the importance of cultivating a spirit of criticism and analysis, while continuing to develop one's own skills in order to excel in this field.
