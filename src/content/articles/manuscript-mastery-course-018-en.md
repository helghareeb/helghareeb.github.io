**Lecture title: Studying Scholars' Handwriting and the Skills of Distinguishing Between Texts and Manuscripts**

### The Eighteenth Lecture of the Program for Mastering the Sciences of Manuscripts

#### Introduction

Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari opened the lecture with a moving supplication, embodying the importance of combining knowledge with righteous deeds. He stressed that this program forms a scholarly pillar that helps researchers explore and analyze the manuscript (makhtut) heritage in depth. The Shaykh took up the study of the relevant chapter of the book *Ma'rifat Khutut al-A'lam fi al-Makhtut al-'Arabi: Qawa'id wa-Namadhij* (Knowing the Handwriting of Eminent Scholars in the Arabic Manuscript: Rules and Models) by Dr. Muhammad ibn Abdullah al-Suray', showing how the book is considered a reference for the methods of distinguishing between hands (khutut) and interpreting them.

### Elements of the Lecture

#### 1. The Importance of Attributing a Hand to Its Writer

- The Shaykh emphasized that establishing the attribution of a hand to the original author is a foundation of scholarly critical editing (tahqiq).
- He explained that a confirmed attribution supports the reliability of texts and drives studies toward accurate results.
- He warned against errors in attributing hands, pointing to their repercussions on scholarly interpretations, since errors may end up being ascribed to the original texts.

#### 2. Factors Affecting the Soundness of Attributing a Hand

- **Explicit statements**: such as the explicit mention of the name of the writer or author.
- **Analysis of handwriting patterns**: comparing different copies to uncover recurring patterns.
- **Material and scholarly evidence**: the importance of the handwriting as evidence of the scholarly and material value of texts.
- The Shaykh stressed that a sound attribution requires comprehensive and precise investigation, relying on a set of interconnected pieces of evidence.

#### 3. Rules for Deducing the Soundness of Attributing Hands

- The Shaykh discussed the scholarly rules set out in the book, pointing to the need to put them into effect in practice.
- He presented the study of the handwriting of Ibn al-Subki as a model for analyzing texts.
- He pointed out that the changes between first copies and later copies reflect developments in the handwriting and the content.

#### 4. Benefits of Recognizing Scholars' Handwriting

- Making it easier to verify the authenticity of texts.
- Determining correct titles and correcting errors of attribution.
- Discovering unrecorded additions and annotations.
- Offering new dimensions for understanding heritage texts by analyzing the circumstances of writing and the features of the hands.

#### 5. Problems Facing Researchers

- Similarity of hands across generations, or between students and their teachers.
- Ambiguity of some expressions in the closing notes (al-quyud al-khitamiyya).
- The need for caution in dealing with texts to avoid mistaken conclusions.
- The Shaykh stressed that overcoming these problems requires deep understanding and wide research experience.

#### 6. Examples from the Book

- The Shaykh pointed to prominent models, such as the handwriting of al-Hafiz Ibn Hajar, and how analyzing it can raise the scholarly and material value of texts.
- He discussed the phenomenon of transcription (naql) as one of the causes of errors, emphasizing the need for verification using reliable evidence.

#### 7. Explicit Statements as Evidence

- The Shaykh explained that explicit statements are the most prominent evidence for proving the soundness of an attribution.
- He stressed the importance of combining other pieces of evidence with explicit statements to strengthen the results.
- He called for using explicit statements within their historical context for a deeper and more precise understanding.

#### 8. Developing Research in the Study of Manuscripts

- The Shaykh emphasized that the study of hands extends to understanding the history of texts and the paths of their circulation.
- He urged researchers to broaden the scope of their study to include the hands of non-eminent figures.
- He stressed the importance of using modern techniques in analyzing manuscripts to achieve more accurate results.

#### The Shaykh's Recommendations

- Read the book carefully and study it again to understand the rules more deeply.
- Develop research skills and the practical application of the methodologies.
- Follow a precise methodology in analyzing evidence and documenting results.
- Contribute to publishing and critically editing texts in a reliable scholarly manner.
- Maintain the continuity of research and development in the sciences of manuscripts.

### Conclusion

The Shaykh concluded the lecture by stressing the importance of going deeper into this vital science and rereading texts carefully to achieve a deeper understanding. He praised the efforts of Dr. Muhammad al-Suray' in enriching the field, emphasizing the importance of improving the presentation of manuscript images in the future. The Shaykh urged participants to continue their study and work toward distinguished scholarly contributions in this specialization.
