**Lecture title: Gaining Practical Expertise in Recognizing the Handwriting of Eminent Scholars in Arabic Manuscripts**

### Introduction

This lecture addressed a highly important subject in the sciences of manuscripts: analyzing the handwriting (khutut) of eminent scholars in Arabic manuscripts (makhtutat). Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari pointed out that this field requires a tight blend of precise theoretical knowledge and in-depth practical experience. Theoretical knowledge is the conceptual foundation for understanding the nature of manuscripts, while practical experience reinforces these concepts and turns them into real skills that enable the researcher to decipher hands and establish their attribution accurately.

### Main Elements of the Lecture

#### The Importance of Practical Application in Mastering the Verification of Hands

The Shaykh stressed that practical experience is the most important element in building the faculty (malaka) of distinguishing between different hands. Without practical experience, theoretical rules remain mere tools that are never activated. He emphasized the need for direct engagement with manuscripts by analyzing their fine details and comparing them with reliable models whose attribution is established.

#### Common Errors in Editing Manuscripts

The Shaykh discussed some of the errors that editors fall into, such as relying on a single weak piece of evidence to attribute a manuscript to its author. He gave as an example what happened in the edition of the book *Jana al-Jinas* by al-Suyuti, where some relied on superficial interpretations of the handwriting, which led to a mistaken attribution of the manuscript. He pointed to the importance of adopting a comprehensive methodology that combines the study of the author's style with the handwriting patterns prevalent in his era.

#### Strategies for Verifying the Attribution of Manuscripts

- **Internal evidence**: relies on analyzing the internal texts of the manuscript, such as style and language, to uncover precise information about the author.
- **Established models**: using known copies whose attribution to the author is confirmed as a basis for comparison.
- **The general pattern of hands**: recalling the handwriting characteristics associated with authors through in-depth study of historical hands.

#### Dealing with Manuscripts of Unknown Author and Title

The Shaykh spoke about how to deal with manuscripts that lack the author's name or the title. He explained that in such cases scholars relied on precise deductive skills based on analyzing writing patterns and styles and comparing them with the characteristics of other texts.

#### Integrating Theory with Practice to Achieve Mastery

The Shaykh pointed out that combining theoretical knowledge with practical application is the path to a comprehensive and deep understanding of manuscripts. While theory provides the intellectual framework, practice consolidates this framework and turns it into tangible skills. The Shaykh stressed that there are elements of knowledge that can only be absorbed through direct engagement with texts.

### The Shaykh's Recommendations to Editors

- **Patience and perseverance**: the Shaykh emphasized that editing manuscripts is a process that requires accumulating experience through time and effort.
- **Focusing on fine details**: he stressed that small details often hold important keys to understanding texts and their attribution.
- **Learning from mistakes**: he pointed out that mistakes are a natural part of the learning process and should be used to improve performance.
- **Treating each manuscript as a unique case**: there is no single rule that applies to all manuscripts, which calls for dealing with each manuscript through a tailored methodology.

### The Study of Hands as a Key to Understanding the Author's Personality

The Shaykh stressed that the study of hands goes beyond merely determining the attribution of manuscripts, reaching to the disclosure of the author's personality and style. Handwriting is a reflection of the writer's thought and cultural context. The Shaykh added that studying the history of Arabic scripts opens a wide field for academic research, since it can contribute to understanding the development of writing and its influence on Islamic civilization.

### Conclusion

The Shaykh concluded the lecture by stressing the importance of sincerity in seeking knowledge and in action, praying that Allah make these efforts purely for His noble Countenance. He explained that the sciences of manuscripts represent a great trust that requires mastery and precision in dealing with them, given their great impact on preserving the Islamic heritage and enriching knowledge for coming generations. He also urged researchers to adhere to a precise scholarly methodology and to focus on continuous development in order to acquire new skills that strengthen their abilities in this vital field.
