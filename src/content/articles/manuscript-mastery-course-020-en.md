### Lecture Title: The Method of Analyzing Manuscript Texts and Documenting Ownership and Reading Notes

#### Introduction

The lecture opened by highlighting the pivotal importance of analyzing manuscript texts as a principal tool in the field of critically editing (tahqiq) the heritage. In the introduction to his talk, Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari addressed the pressing need to develop analytical skills and to commit to scholarly assignments, stressing that manuscripts (makhtutat) are not only sources of historical knowledge but living witnesses to the development of the sciences and cultures of Islamic civilization. Analyzing these texts embodies a multidisciplinary scholarly project that calls for mastery of theoretical and practical tools to understand them.

#### Main Elements

### 1. The Importance of Analyzing Manuscript Texts

- **Analyzing titles**: analyzing titles is a pivotal step in understanding the nature of the text and determining the identity of the copy (nuskha). Titles often show indications relating to the date of copying, its place, and perhaps the hands of the copyists (nussakh) who prepared the copy.
- **Proving or disproving the attribution of the text**: analyzing titles helps to prove or disprove their attribution to the author, an essential step in the context of scholarly verification.
- **The effect of copying on texts**: copies reveal the extent to which texts were affected by the changing hands that transmitted them, highlighting the differences and modifications that occurred over time.
- **Classifying titles**: distinguishing between main titles and subtitles provides a deeper view of how texts were handled in their different historical contexts.

### 2. Tools for Analyzing Manuscript Copies

- **Identifying the type of script**: analyzing the type of script (khatt) helps reveal the authenticity of texts, by determining whether they are in the author's hand, the copyists' hands, or later hands.
- **Examining introductory phrases**: phrases such as "taghammadahu Allah bi-rahmatih" (may Allah envelop him in His mercy) provide chronological clues that help determine when the copying took place.
- **Modern techniques**: digital techniques, such as analyzing images with advanced software, are an essential tool for improving the legibility of texts and interpreting difficult symbols.
- **Distinguishing marginal notes from the original texts**: this procedure makes it possible to understand the interpretive or explanatory contexts that were added later.

### 3. Documenting Ownership and Reading Notes

- **Analyzing ownership notes (tamallukat)**: studying phrases such as "malakahu fulan" (so-and-so owned it) reveals the passage of texts between owners, providing a historical map of the stages of the text's circulation.
- **Ordering ownership notes**: arranging ownership notes chronologically is an effective tool for tracing the historical and geographical path of texts.
- **The importance of reading notes (mutala'at)**: reading notes add another dimension to the study of texts, since they show the level of scholarly interest and how widely the texts spread among scholars.
- **Comparative analysis**: comparing the multiple ownership notes on the same text helps reveal the possible influence of each owner on the text.

### 4. Applied Examples of Ownership Notes

- Studying specific examples such as "malakahu Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Ghazzi" (Muhammad ibn Muhammad al-Ghazzi owned it) and "malakahu 'Abd al-'Aziz al-Qurashi" ('Abd al-'Aziz al-Qurashi owned it) to show how the path of texts can be inferred.
- Analyzing the passage of copies between prominent libraries, such as the Kashif al-Ghita' Library and the Chester Beatty Library, which reflects the cultural and geographical importance of the texts.
- Inferring the effect of the different owners' ownership on the texts and the extent to which they were modified or preserved.

### 5. The Importance of Hands and Annotations in the Manuscript

- **Analyzing marginal notes (hawashi)**: marginal notes written in the hands of copyists or scholars help provide a rich context for understanding texts.
- **Methods of precision and vocalization (dabt and tashkil)**: analyzing precision and vocalization shows the copyists' accuracy and the extent of their adherence to the original.
- **Understanding symbols and marks**: studying the symbols used, such as the circles separating biographical entries, is part of understanding the contexts.
- **Multiple hands**: analyzing texts written in multiple hands helps in understanding the extent of the copyists' intervention and changes to the texts.

### 6. Methods of Reading and Analysis

- **Repetition as an analytical tool**: repeated reading of unclear texts helps clarify their meanings and details.
- **Using indexes and catalogues**: indexes, catalogues (faharis) and references are essential tools for understanding texts and their surrounding contexts.
- **Digital techniques**: tools such as Photoshop make it possible to improve the legibility of texts and analyze them more precisely.
- **Analyzing hands**: distinguishing the fine differences between hands helps in tracing modifications.

### 7. Dealing with Dates and Lineages

- **Verifying dates**: comparing the dates mentioned in the texts with historical sources to verify their accuracy.
- **Analyzing lineages**: studying the lineages (ansab) found in the texts helps connect texts to their owners or authors.
- **Textual comparison**: comparing texts in different hands makes it possible to identify the original texts.
- **Historical contexts**: understanding the historical context of each period adds a rich dimension to the analysis of texts.

### Conclusion

The lecture stressed that analyzing manuscript texts is an interconnected scholarly specialization requiring precision, patience and knowledge of historical contexts. This work is a central axis in preserving cultural heritage and presenting it to coming generations accurately and reliably.

Manuscript texts are not merely historical documents; they are treasures of knowledge carrying within them the development of human thought. Editing them requires combining modern techniques with traditional skills to ensure the preservation of this heritage.

### Recommendations

- **Precise documentation**: the need to document ownership and reading notes, with reference to their contexts.
- **Continuing study**: continuing to study texts using modern methods.
- **Scholarly cooperation**: strengthening cooperation with researchers and specialists in editing texts.
- **Workshops**: organizing advanced workshops to improve analytical skills.
- **Creating a digital archive**: building a digital database that makes it easy to refer back to the observations drawn from the texts.

With this orientation, the lecture highlights the importance of combining academic depth with practical application in analyzing texts, contributing to a deeper understanding of the human heritage.
