### Main Title: The Scholarly Analysis of Manuscript Copies and the Rules of Documentation in the Final Lesson

#### Introduction

This lesson opened with a supplication to Allah, Glorified and Exalted is He, that the scholarly work be purely for His noble Countenance and bring lasting benefit to researchers in the science of manuscripts. In this final lecture, Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari addressed the analysis of manuscript copies (nusakh khattiyya) with an advanced methodology combining academic precision and practical application. The importance of scholarly integrity as a fundamental pillar of manuscript study was highlighted, along with a review of the great benefits that can be achieved by applying this methodology.

#### Main Themes

- **The importance of analyzing texts in manuscript copies**:
  - The lesson went into detail on how to analyze the texts written on the title pages and closing pages, since these texts are considered a key to understanding the context of the copy (nuskha).
  - The focus was on studying the characteristics of the copyist's (nasikh) hand, while verifying the consistency between the main texts and any later additions.
  - Attention was drawn to the importance of the supplication written in the copy, since it is used as an analytical tool to determine whether the author was alive or dead at the time of copying.
  - The Shaykh highlighted how these methods can strengthen the reliability of texts and make them primary sources in intellectual and historical studies.
- **Precise observations on analyzing texts**:
  - The Shaykh explained the ways of examining the supplication found in the copy, distinguishing between supplications for mercy, which indicate that the author had died, and those praying for his continued life, which indicate that he was alive.
  - Light was shed on the marginal notes (hawashi) and comments written in the margins, since they are a fundamental element for understanding the historical and cultural context of the copy.
  - Attention was drawn to the importance of analyzing textual differences between the original copy and the additions, explaining the role of tables or additional comments in revealing the history of texts.
  - Practical examples were presented showing how these analyses can reveal the modifications the copy underwent over time.
- **Practical discussions and applying the methodologies**:
  - The Shaykh led applied discussions with the students on several manuscript copies, such as the copy of Imam Muhammad ibn Saud University and the copy of Shaykh Sa'd. These discussions included analyzing writing patterns and the terminology used.
  - The importance of group interaction in studying texts was stressed, since discussions are an effective educational tool for developing analytical skills.
  - The discussions revealed texts whose analysis was incomplete, such as marginal notes and tables, which led to an emphasis on the importance of comprehensiveness in the work.
  - Practical examples were given of interpreting textual differences and linking them to historical contexts, which strengthened the applied understanding of the material.
- **Final recommendations and corrections**:
  - The Shaykh called for returning to the original texts as the primary reference for any analytical study, warning of the dangers of relying on secondary copies.
  - The need to review the dates found in the texts and compare them with the authors' dates to ensure the soundness of the copy was emphasized.
  - He encouraged the students to improve their academic documentation, so that notes are precise and organized, making them easier to review and analyze later.
  - He drew attention to the importance of using modern tools to analyze copies, including specialized software that helps compare texts more effectively.
- **Conclusion of the lesson and future guidance**:
  - The Shaykh concluded the session by stressing the importance of scholarly integrity and its pivotal role in the field of manuscript editing (tahqiq).
  - He apologized for postponing some lectures, noting his full commitment to making them up as soon as possible.
  - He asked those present for understanding and forgiveness if circumstances prevented holding additional meetings.
  - The lesson closed with a call to continue scholarly research in the field of manuscripts, noting that this field is still full of opportunities to discover intellectual and historical treasures.

#### Summary

The lesson offered an in-depth analysis of texts in manuscript copies in a scholarly and practical manner, focusing on the importance of precision and documentation in this field. Shaykh Salih al-Azhari highlighted the pivotal role of group interaction in improving the quality of research, with commitment to scholarly integrity as the basis for the continuity of this science. Finally, emphasis was placed on making use of modern tools to strengthen research capabilities and broaden the horizons of manuscript study.
