### Lesson Title: The Critical Analysis of Titles and Texts in the Science of Manuscript Editing

#### Introduction

Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari began the lesson by praising and thanking Allah, with a sincere supplication that this scholarly gathering be a means of attaining beneficial knowledge and righteous deeds. He stressed the importance of a sound intention as the basis of any scholarly effort, emphasizing the need for patience and perseverance to achieve tangible progress in academic study. He also pointed out that acquiring knowledge is not limited to collecting information but requires deep understanding and a comprehensive conception.

#### Content of the Lesson

- **The methodology of critical reading:**
  - The Shaykh explained the importance of repeated critical reading as a means of understanding the different dimensions of scholarly texts. He explained that reading is an analytical process that breaks ideas down and rebuilds them in a more mature and clearer way.
  - He advised researchers to adopt an analytical methodology that focuses on the pivotal points and on the differences between issues, enabling them to build a coherent knowledge base.
  - He emphasized the need to diversify one's reading sources to ensure a comprehensive view of the various subjects, which broadens perception and deepens understanding.
- **Problems of titles and their analysis:**
  - The Shaykh addressed the issue of multiple titles in manuscripts (makhtutat), explaining that differences in naming often reflect the stages of a book's circulation or changes in social and historical circumstances.
  - He gave a practical example of al-Nasa'i's book *al-Sunan*, pointing to its different names such as *al-Mujtaba* and *al-Sunan al-Ma'thura*, focusing on analyzing the difference between subject titles that express the structure of the book and descriptive titles that reflect its general content.
- **Criteria for scholarly preference among titles:**
  - The Shaykh reviewed precise criteria for assessing titles, such as examining internal texts like introductions and chapter headings, which carry direct indications of the title.
  - He pointed to the importance of relying on reliable quotations from earlier scholars such as al-Suyuti and Ibn Hajar, who are considered pivotal sources.
  - He stressed that the final judgment on a title must rest on the integration of textual and paleographic evidence, with a critical analysis that takes into account all available indications.
- **Analyzing problems of quotations:**
  - The Shaykh discussed the phenomenon of some scholars relying on quotations without verifying the original texts, which leads to contradictions or errors of interpretation.
  - He offered methodological solutions to these problems, including verifying the context of the quotation, comparing it with the original texts, and analyzing the credibility of the source.
  - He emphasized that scholarly criticism requires a deeper understanding of the causes of errors, together with offering methodological alternatives to improve results.
- **Building an overall conception of the science:**
  - The Shaykh stressed that an overall conception of the science is the first step toward comprehensive understanding. He explained that this conception helps the researcher understand the links between different issues.
  - He advised researchers to create mind maps to clarify the basic concepts and the relationships between them, updating them periodically on the basis of acquired knowledge.
  - He explained that an overall conception gives the researcher a greater capacity for critical analysis and comparison between texts.
- **Scholarly criticism as an advanced stage:**
  - The Shaykh emphasized that scholarly criticism represents the summit of research skills and requires precise analysis and advanced evaluation skills.
  - He pointed out that criticism is not limited to identifying errors but includes offering interpretations and alternatives supported by methodological evidence.
  - He recommended training in criticism through studying real cases and using applied examples, which strengthens the researcher's ability to deal with different texts.
- **The importance of practical application:**
  - The Shaykh pointed out that practical application is the most effective means of consolidating theoretical understanding. He encouraged the students to begin small projects of editing texts.
  - He explained that interacting with specialists helps reduce errors and assists in gaining valuable experience.
  - He stressed that perseverance in facing practical challenges is an essential part of developing research skills.

#### Plan for the Future Program

- **Choosing editing topics:**
  - A topic will be chosen for each student based on his inclinations and specialization, taking into account a balance in the level of difficulty.
  - The Shaykh explained that this guidance aims to ensure focused efforts and high-quality results.
- **Timeline:**
  - A strict timeline will be set for completing the edition within two months, with regular follow-up sessions.
  - The Shaykh pointed out that adhering to this timeline is an essential element in ensuring the project's success.
- **Supervision and guidance:**
  - A specialized team will supervise all stages of the editing, from choosing the topic to completing the final review.
  - Group discussion sessions will be held to exchange experience and strengthen collaborative learning.
- **Publishing the scholarly works:**
  - The final works will be published under the students' names, with the necessary support provided to complete the printing and publishing process to the highest quality standards.
  - The Shaykh explained that this step is not merely the crowning of the work but a gateway to opening new horizons for scholarly research.

#### Conclusion

Shaykh Salih al-Azhari concluded the lesson by urging the students to commit to hard work and perseverance in order to achieve distinguished scholarly output. He stressed that this program is a leading platform for developing research skills and opening new horizons in the field of manuscript editing. He prayed that Allah grant everyone success in their scholarly endeavors, emphasizing that sincerity and seriousness are the foundation of any academic achievement.
