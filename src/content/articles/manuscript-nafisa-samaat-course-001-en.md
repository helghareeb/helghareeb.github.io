## Lecture Title: Surveying Audition Lists and Analyzing the Structural Elements of Audition Certificates

### Lecture Summary

**1. Introduction:**

- Shaykh Salih Muhammad Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari began the lecture by praising Allah and invoking blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him).
- The Shaykh stressed that the present course complements what was studied in the previous course, as this course focuses on the practical, applied side of manuscript studies.
- The Shaykh explained the method of the course, which is based on reading samples and analyzing them to extract their structural elements.

**2. Aims of the Course:**

- Testing how well the trainees have absorbed what was taught in the previous courses.
- Training the trainees to read audition certificates (sama'at) accurately and to analyze them into their basic elements.
- Moving from reading to the skill of inference and deduction in order to benefit from manuscript texts.

**3. Content of the Lecture:**

- Reading and analyzing selected samples of audition lists (tabaqat al-sama').
- Discussing the errors editors fall into when reading manuscripts and analyzing texts.
- Presenting applied examples that show how to analyze audition certificates and draw out what they indicate.

**4. Main Topics:**

- **The importance of understanding the scholarly structure of audition certificates:**
  - The scholarly structure of audition certificates includes several elements, such as the reference to the chain of transmission (sanad), the mention of the audition lists, and the way texts are linked together.
  - Every word and every blank space in manuscript texts carries a meaning that must be properly understood.
- **Correcting common errors:**
  - Correcting errors in reading texts, such as confusion in personal names or in the expressions used.
  - Explaining how to deal with the recurring terms found in manuscripts.
- **Inferences drawn from audition lists:**
  - The importance of identifying the roles of those present at the audition (such as the reader (qari') and the writer).
  - Linking texts together to clarify the relationship between the chain of transmission and the text (matn).

**5. Applied Examples:**

- Reading texts that contain problems, such as inverted expressions.
- Analyzing the texts of copied-over audition certificates and comparing them with the original certificates.
- Clarifying the editor's role in dealing with manuscript texts so as to preserve their scholarly credibility.

**6. Recommendations to the Trainees:**

- Reviewing the previous lectures on audition certificates to understand the basics of analysis.
- Practicing the reading of manuscript texts continuously to improve one's skills.
- Focusing on the small details in texts, such as the ink, the blank spaces and the symbols used.

**7. Closing:**

- The Shaykh stressed the importance of active participation during the course in order to benefit from it as fully as possible.
- He urged the trainees to put more effort into studying manuscripts and understanding their elements.

**Summary:** The lecture offered a comprehensive practical introduction to dealing with manuscript audition certificates and analyzing their elements. It also made clear the importance of continuous practice and a precise understanding of the scholarly structure of texts in order to produce an edition that is complete and reliable.
