### Lesson Title: Understanding the Audition List and the Chain of Transmission in Arabic Manuscripts

### Elements of the Lesson

- **Defining the audition list and the chain of transmission:**
  - **The audition list (tabaqat al-sama'):** a certificate establishing that an individual attended a particular audition session, in which he received the book from a particular shaykh on a particular date.
  - **The chain of transmission (isnad):** the series of narrators linking the owner of the copy to the original author, made up of each shaykh naming his own shaykh until the author is reached.
- **The difference between the audition and the chain of transmission:**
  - The audition concerns the actual attestation of the session in which the text was received.
  - The chain of transmission is the historical sequence of narrators.
- **The importance of the audition list:**
  - It is considered proof that the book was received directly from the shaykh.
  - It is used to document texts and to analyze the soundness of copies.
- **Types of audition:**
  - **Original audition:** based on an actual attestation of the session in which the audition took place.
  - **Copied-over audition:** a copy derived from the original audition, which may require further analysis to confirm that the information is sound.
- **Problems in editing manuscripts:**
  - Relying on a copied-over audition may lead to errors in assessing the value of texts.
  - Example: the attribution of the handwriting of a copy to al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman al-Muradi and treating the copy as being in his hand, which led to errors in editing the text.
- **How to benefit from the audition list:**
  - Determining whether the audition is original before using it to establish the date of the copy or its attribution.
  - Avoiding building scholarly conclusions on copied-over auditions.
- **Practical examples:**
  - Discussing the case of the copy of *al-Risala* by Imam al-Shafi'i, and analyzing the problem of attributing its handwriting to al-Rabi' ibn Sulayman.

### Closing Notes

- The importance of precision in analyzing the audition list and the chain of transmission to avoid problems in scholarly editing.
- The need to distinguish between original and copied-over auditions to avoid errors in attributing texts.
