### Lesson Title: Tracing Auditions and Copied Manuscripts - Methods and Practical Lessons

### Lecture Summary

### 1. An Introduction to Correcting Errors in Manuscripts

- Shaykh Salih spoke about the importance of understanding the methods of copyists (nussakh), which include omitting the hamza and not dotting words whose reading is obvious.
- He discussed an example of a misreading of a manuscript in which the phrase "sahib Juz' Ibn Fil" (the transmitter of *Juz' Ibn Fil*) was distorted (tahrif) into "sahib hurr Ibn Fid", which showed the importance of precision in reading manuscripts.

### 2. The Editor's Acumen and Its Role in Overcoming Errors

- The Shaykh stressed the need for acumen (fitna) in editing texts, citing the saying of Ibn al-Salah: "Acumen is among the best things a person has been given."
- He discussed how the editor can overcome obstacles through a precise and methodical understanding of the manuscript.

### 3. The Importance of Practical Exercises in the Field of Editing

- The Shaykh spoke of his personal experience in the manuscript library and the effect of practice in developing skills.
- He stressed the importance of recording personal notes in order to build the faculty (malaka) of editing.

### 4. Analyzing Audition Lists

- The Shaykh focused on how to identify the persons connected with an audition through their audition lists (tabaqat al-sama').
- He explained the method of following the end of the thread from one list to another, such as the list of "Ali ibn Mas'ud ibn Nafis al-Mawsili", and how to link copied copies with original ones.

### 5. Rules and Rulings on Children's Audition

- The Shaykh addressed the differences of opinion over the age at which audition is valid, citing the words of Imam al-Dhahabi, who sets presence (hudur) for children at age five and above.

### 6. Methods of Transfer Between Copies

- The Shaykh pointed to the ways in which material was transferred between copies, distinguishing between copied copies and original copies, and explained the importance of transfer in documentation.

### 7. Copying Errors and Methods of Review

- He spoke about copyists' errors in personal names, such as "al-Shiraji" and "al-Siraji", and the need to use biographical dictionaries to verify them.
- He recommended using programs such as al-Maktaba al-Shamila to make searching easier and to confirm that names are correct.

### 8. Copied Copies Among Original Copies

- He showed the importance of understanding the relationship between copied-over lists and original lists, and explained how the editor can distinguish between copies by comparing the handwriting or by determining the purpose of the copying.

### 9. The Importance of Group Work and Interaction

- The Shaykh stressed the importance of practical sessions and group exercises for a deeper understanding of the sciences of audition and editing.

### Main Points for Study

- Methods of dealing with errors in manuscript texts.
- Principles of the editor's acumen and its effect on editing texts.
- Analyzing audition lists: original and copied-over.
- The legal and historical criteria for children's audition.
- Techniques for verifying names using biographical dictionaries.
- The importance of understanding transfer between copies to ensure a sound edition.

### Recommendations

- Reviewing the lecture more than once to grasp the fine issues the Shaykh touched upon.
- Applying the advice in practice to sample manuscripts.
- Studying biographical dictionaries in depth to understand how to deal with the names of historical figures.
- Practicing the analysis of audition lists and linking them together to understand the methods of documentation and audition.
