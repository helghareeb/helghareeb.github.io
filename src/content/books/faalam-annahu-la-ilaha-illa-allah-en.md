## About the book

“La ilaha illa Allah” is the greatest word and the first obligation upon the servant, and it is the key to Paradise; yet a key opens only with its teeth. This book on the oneness of worship (*tawhid al-uluhiyyah*) begins from the opening of the Verse of the Throne, “Allah — there is no god except Him” (Al-Baqarah 2:255; translation of the meaning). It explains the meaning of the word and its eight conditions, the Prophet’s protection of its sanctity, and shirk and the ways it enters the heart, and closes with the fruits of tawhid in this world, the barzakh and the Hereafter. Its chapters include figures and mind maps that gather its meanings.

- **Volume 1 (355 pages):** the introduction and Parts One and Two.
- **Volume 2 (397 pages):** Parts Three and Four, the conclusion, and the list of sources.

## Method

- Questions are set out according to the method of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama‘ah, with evidence from the Qur’an, the Sunnah and the reports of the Salaf, and from the creed works transmitted with chains.
- Hadiths are referenced to their sources, with the grading of a recognised authority for anything outside the two Sahihs; weak reports are not used as proof.
- Scholars are quoted in their own words, referenced to volume and page of a named edition.
- Verses are written in the ‘Uthmani script with their surahs, and every quotation and reference is checked against its source.
