This repository contains the LaTeX source and compiled PDF of a comprehensive textbook on
information storage and retrieval, written for the graduate course at the Faculty of Computers
and Information Sciences, Mansoura University. Each chapter closes with multiple-choice
questions, and the appendices add an essay question bank with answer sketches and hands-on labs.
