## About the book

The book addresses the first-year university student and treats the move from school to university as a
change in how one thinks and in what responsibility means, not merely a change of place or timetable.

It grows out of direct academic and teaching experience in university classrooms and out of the
questions students themselves ask at the start: how to learn rather than memorise, how to manage time
and freedom when no one is watching, how to build relationships and a presence in a diverse university
community, and how to turn a specialisation into a mission with lasting effect.

It does not offer quick recipes for success. It builds a rounded awareness along six axes that join the
skills of a student of knowledge to their ethics, and the intellectual side of a student's formation to
the human and moral side.

## The six parts

- **Self-awareness:** discovering oneself, building identity and inner discipline.
- **Academic awareness:** understanding how learning works, and building the tools and ethics of a student.
- **Social awareness:** managing relationships and emotions, and a balanced presence in university society.
- **Faith awareness:** purifying the heart and keeping the bond with Allah amid busyness and trials.
- **Practical awareness:** turning knowledge into impact, an idea into a project, a specialisation into a mission.
- **Digital awareness:** using technology consciously, so that it stays a useful tool and not a force
  that consumes a person from within.

The book closes with a conclusion and six practical appendices, including a plan for the first ninety
days at university, self-review questions and short practical templates.
