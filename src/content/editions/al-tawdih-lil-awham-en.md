**Author:** the hadith master Abu Dharr Muwaffaq al-Din Ahmad ibn Ibrahim ibn Muhammad ibn Khalil al-Halabi al-Shafi'i, known like his father as Sibt Ibn al-'Ajami (d. 884 AH); his father is Burhan al-Din Abu al-Wafa' Ibrahim Sibt Ibn al-'Ajami (d. 841 AH), author of *al-Talqih li-Fahm Qari' al-Sahih*.

**Edited by** Dr. Haitham A. El-Ghareeb, under the supervision and review of Shaykh Salih ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Fattah al-Azhari.

**Published** jointly by al-Azhari Manuscript Studies Courses, al-Warraqa al-Ula Foundation for Educational Consultancy, and Dar al-Imam Muslim.

The work examines variants, scribal corruptions and errors in the wording, names, numbers, dates and places found in the transmissions and copies of *Sahih al-Bukhari*, explaining their causes and weighing one reading against another. The text is preceded by an introductory study of the main transmitters of the *Sahih*, Abu Dharr al-Harawi, the standing of the Yunini copy, and the causes of variation among its transmissions, followed by a study of the author, the title, the attribution and the manuscript used.
