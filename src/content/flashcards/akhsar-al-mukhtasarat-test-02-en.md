question: "Explain the difference between purifying water (tahur) and pure but non-purifying water (tahir), giving an example of each."
answer: "Purifying water is water that remains in its original natural state and keeps its name, such as unqualified water (al-ma' al-mutlaq), which lifts ritual impurity and removes filth. Pure but non-purifying water is water mixed with a pure substance that has changed its description or its name, such as water that has become broth or tea: it is pure, but ablution (wudu') may not be performed with it."
