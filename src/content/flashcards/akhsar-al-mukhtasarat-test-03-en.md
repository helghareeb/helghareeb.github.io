question: "What follows from the principle that the default ruling on vessels is purity, and what is the most notable exception in this chapter?"
answer: "It follows that vessels of glass, wood, stone, iron and the like remain permissible and pure unless something establishes otherwise. The most notable exception is vessels of gold and silver, and whatever is equivalent to them in forbidden use."
