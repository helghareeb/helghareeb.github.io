question: "Name four sunnahs of ablution (wudu'), noting the case in which washing the hands before wudu' is obligatory rather than sunnah."
answer: "Sunnahs of wudu' include: using the siwak; washing the hands for someone who is not rising from night sleep; rinsing the mouth and nose (madmadah and istinshaq) first; doing them thoroughly for someone who is not fasting; running wet fingers through the beard and between the fingers (takhlil); and washing each part three times. Washing the hands before wudu' is obligatory for someone rising from night sleep that nullifies wudu'."
