question: "When is it permissible to wipe over a turban, a woman's head covering (khimar), or a splint (jabirah)?"
answer: "Wiping over a turban is permissible if it is wound under the chin (muhannakah) or has a hanging tail (dhu'abah) and is difficult to remove; over a woman's khimar if it is wrapped under her throat and is difficult to remove; and over a splint if it is needed at the site of a fracture, in which case one wipes over it until it is undone."
