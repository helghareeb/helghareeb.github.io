question: "Give an example of the case jurists mention about when the period of wiping over khuffs begins, according to those who count it from the first ritual impurity (hadath)."
answer: "Example: someone puts on his khuffs after performing wudu' for Fajr, then incurs ritual impurity (hadath) in the mid-morning (duha), and does not wipe until Dhuhr. The period is counted from the time of the hadath in the mid-morning, not from the time of wiping at Dhuhr."
