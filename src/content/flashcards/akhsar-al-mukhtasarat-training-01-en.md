question: "What is the difference between change caused by a non-mixing substance and change caused by a mixing substance, and what effect does each have on the ruling of the water?"
answer: "Change by a non-mixing substance is change caused by something adjacent that does not truly mix with the water; this generally does not take the water out of being purifying (tahur). Change by a pure mixing substance is change through mixing that affects the water's name or description; the water then becomes pure but non-purifying (tahir), in the terminology of the text."
