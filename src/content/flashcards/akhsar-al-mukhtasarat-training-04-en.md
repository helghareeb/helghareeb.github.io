question: "What is the general ruling on vessels of gold and silver, and what is the most notable reason given in the commentary?"
answer: "It is not permissible to acquire them or to use them for eating, drinking and the like. Among the reasons mentioned: \"They are for them in this world and for you in the Hereafter\"; and some scholars mentioned that they break the hearts of the poor."
