question: "What defines a thick beard, and how does that affect running the fingers through it (takhlil) and washing it?"
answer: "A thick beard is one that hides the skin: washing its outer surface suffices, and running the fingers through it is recommended (mustahabb). A light beard through which the skin can be seen must be washed both inside and out."
