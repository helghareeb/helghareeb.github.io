question: "Must a splint (jabirah) be put on in a state of purity? And what is the ruling if it extends beyond the area of need?"
answer: "The correct view in the commentary is that it need not be put on in a state of purity, because fractures happen suddenly. If it extends beyond the area of need and removing the excess is feared to cause harm, one wipes over it and performs dry ablution (tayammum) for the excess."
