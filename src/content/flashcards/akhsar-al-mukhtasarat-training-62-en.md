question: "Q5. Underline the intrinsically impure substances:\nhuman leftover water (su'r) · semen (mani) · carrion · pre-seminal fluid (madhy) · the urine of a small child · the leftover water of an animal whose meat is eaten · a dog's saliva · post-urinary discharge (wady) · dead fish and locusts · menstrual blood · wine (khamr) · a cat's leftover water · the carcass of a creature without flowing blood · the urine and feces of an animal whose meat is eaten · the leftover water of predatory animals · a dead human being"
answer: "The intrinsically impure substances here are, in general:\n- carrion, except what has been excepted\n- pre-seminal fluid (madhy)\n- the urine of a small child, before it is cleaned\n- a dog's saliva\n- post-urinary discharge (wady)\n- menstrual blood\n- wine (khamr), according to the well-known view\n- the leftover water of predatory animals, according to those who consider it impure or not purifying\n\nAs for:\n- human leftover water\n- semen, according to the preponderant view\n- the leftover water of an animal whose meat is eaten\n- dead fish and locusts\n- a cat's leftover water\n- the carcass of a creature without flowing blood\n- the urine and feces of an animal whose meat is eaten, in some details of the school\n- a dead human being\nthese are not all counted among the intrinsically impure substances without qualification; some of them involve a difference of opinion and further detail."
