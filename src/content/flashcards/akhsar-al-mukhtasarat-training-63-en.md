question: "Q6. Circle the waters below with which purification is permitted:\nsea water · used water · water into which a pure substance fell and changed its name · a small amount of water into which an impurity fell · well water · a large amount of water changed by an impurity · water slightly changed by a pure substance · water from snow and hail · a large amount of water changed by something impure"
answer: "Purification is permitted here with:\n- sea water\n- well water\n- water from snow and hail, once melted or whenever it can be used\n- water slightly changed by a pure substance, as long as it is still called water and has not lost its unqualified status\n\nAs for:\n- used water (al-ma' al-musta'mal), in the terminology of the text\n- water into which a pure substance fell and changed its name\n- a small amount of water into which an impurity fell\n- a large amount of water changed by an impurity\n- a large amount of water changed by something impure\npurification is not performed with these, according to the well-known classification in this chapter."
