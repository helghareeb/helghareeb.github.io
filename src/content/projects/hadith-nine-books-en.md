An ongoing da'wah and scholarly project that gives every hadith of the nine books its own page: the audio read by Dr. Yusuf bin Nuh Ahmad (with his permission), the vowelled text with its chain, its rare words explained, its grade and reference, a brief explanation and benefits, and an English translation. Selected hadith are also produced as videos for screens, phones and WhatsApp statuses. It begins with the Book of Revelation in Sahih al-Bukhari.
